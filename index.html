About

BGP communities are widely used by network operators to express routing policy, yet their operational use, visibility, and interpretation remain only partially understood.

Communities in the Wild brings together network operators, Internet measurement researchers, and tool developers to discuss operational practice, measurement methodologies, observability, validation, and emerging research directions related to BGP communities.

The workshop will combine invited presentations with moderated discussions and community exchange. Its goal is to strengthen collaboration between the operational routing and Internet measurement communities, identify open measurement challenges, and help shape future research and operational practice.

Participation

The workshop welcomes participation from network operators, Internet exchange operators, CDN and cloud providers, Internet measurement and routing security researchers, geolocation and measurement platform providers, and developers of routing analysis tools.

We particularly encourage participation from network operators interested in sharing operational experience and engaging with the research community. The workshop is intended to foster discussion and exchange between practitioners and researchers working on BGP communities.

Participation details and any registration requirements will be posted here once they are confirmed by ACM IMC 2026.
